# Nutrition Plan — Brian Rand

## Targets
- **Calories:** 2,500–3,000/day (bulking)
- **Protein:** High priority for training load and recovery
- **Meal timing:** Optimized around 12+ hour work days

## Dietary Constraints
- ❌ Dairy (lactose intolerant)
- ❌ High-purine proteins (gout trigger)
- ❌ Processed meats (cholesterol + gout)
- ❌ Refined flour (prediabetic)
- ❌ Excess saturated fat (high cholesterol)

## Recommended Foods
- **Protein:** Egg whites, plant protein powder, low-purine fish, chicken breast
- **Anti-inflammatory:** Tart cherry juice, turmeric, ginger, berries
- **Carbs:** Steel-cut oats, sweet potato, quinoa, low-glycemic options
- **Fats:** Avocado, nuts, olive oil
- **Fiber:** Vegetables, legumes (watch purine content)
