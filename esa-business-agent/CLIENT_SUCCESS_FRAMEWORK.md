This is the shared brain for Event Sales Agency's Client Success team. Every client we work with gets their own chat inside this project. This is where we run onboarding, build custom roadmaps, write client communications, track sprint progress, troubleshoot fulfillment, and maintain a consistent, high-quality experience across every client relationship.

The framework in these instructions is the BASE. Every single client gets a CUSTOM version built from their intake form and kickoff call. Nothing here is boilerplate that goes directly to a client — it is the starting point that gets tailored to their event, their team, their goals, and their timeline.

Every team member using this project operates from the same playbooks, voice, and standards. When in doubt, reference these instructions before doing anything.

## THE CLIENT ONBOARDING FLOW

### STEP 1 — Payment Confirmed (Automated, Day 0)
**Trigger:** Stripe payment processes → GHL fires welcome sequence
- Welcome email sent (includes Brian video)
- Slack channel created: [FirstName]-ESA
- Onboarding intake form link sent to client
- Kim tagged to confirm receipt and monitor form completion
- **SLA:** Kickoff call must be scheduled within 48 hours of payment

### STEP 2 — Onboarding Intake Form (Client completes, 48–72 hrs)
Client must complete this before the kickoff call is scheduled. Kim holds this gate — no form, no call.

**Form must collect:**
- Event name, date, location (virtual/in-person/hybrid)
- Ticket price(s) and tiers (GA, VIP, etc.)
- Revenue goal and ticket quantity goal
- Current ad account access (yes/no, platform)
- Existing audience size (email list, social following)
- Existing leads or past attendees (warm lists)
- Team members who will be using the system
- Brand assets (logo, colors, fonts — uploaded or Drive link)
- Top 3 challenges they want to solve
- What does success look like at Day 90?

### STEP 3 — Kickoff Strategy Call (Denise/Ops Manager runs, Fathom records)
60-minute structured discovery call. **Agenda:**
1. Welcome + relationship building (5 min)
2. Confirm event details and timeline (10 min)
3. Review intake form — go deeper on goals and constraints (15 min)
4. Map their event math: tickets needed × price = revenue goal (10 min)
5. Identify the custom elements: what's unique about this client vs. the base framework (10 min)
6. Walk through what we're building and when (7 min)
7. Set communication expectations: Slack response times, weekly calls, updates (3 min)

**After the call:** paste the Fathom transcript into the client's chat inside this project with the prompt: "Build the custom client roadmap from this transcript using our 90-day sprint framework."

### STEP 4 — Custom Roadmap Build (Ops Manager + James, 24–48 hrs post-call)
This is not boilerplate. The roadmap is built specifically for this client based on their intake form and Fathom transcript. It includes:
- Their 90-day sprint milestones customized to their event date and goals
- Specific deliverables by phase with owner assigned
- Their ticket goal and event math baked in
- Custom elements noted explicitly (the parts that deviate from the base framework)
- Which GHL snapshot variation is being used and why

**Roadmap goes into:**
1. Their ClickUp workspace as the master project plan
2. Pinned message in their Slack channel

### STEP 5 — Client Roadmap Sign-Off (Required before build starts)
Client responds "approved" or equivalent in Slack. Kim logs the sign-off date in ClickUp. The build does not start until this is confirmed. This is scope protection — once approved, this roadmap is the contract.

### STEP 6 — ClickUp Workspace Built from Approved Roadmap
Kim creates the ClickUp project from the signed roadmap — not from a generic template. Every task is named with the actual deliverable for this client. Client has view access. They can see real-time progress at any time.

### STEP 7 — Sprint Execution
Shah/GHL team pulls from ClickUp tasks. Ops Manager runs weekly check-in calls. Kim sends async Slack updates 3x per week (Mon/Wed/Fri). Each phase has a milestone the client signs off on before the next phase begins.

## THE 90-DAY SPRINT
The goal is simple: build everything as fast as possible, hand it over completely, and get the client winning on their own. We are not a retainer. We are not babysitters. We build the system, train the team, and exit clean.

This framework is the base. Every client gets a custom version of this sprint built from their intake form and kickoff call. Timelines compress or expand based on client responsiveness — a client who shows up fast gets their system faster. The faster we deliver, the better the client result. Speed is the service.

### Phase 1 — Foundation (Days 1–14)
**Goal:** Everything we need to build is locked and approved before we touch GHL.
- Onboarding intake form completed by client
- Kickoff strategy call completed and Fathom recorded
- Custom roadmap built from Fathom transcript + intake data
- Client signs off on roadmap in Slack
- GHL sub-account created, correct snapshot selected and installed
- ClickUp workspace built from approved roadmap (client has view access)
- Event math model completed (tickets needed × price = revenue goal)
- 100-point event checklist completed

**Milestone gate:** Client approves roadmap → build begins. Nothing starts before this.

### Phase 2 — System Build (Days 15–35)
**Goal:** Full GHL system live and QA'd. Client can log in and see everything working.
- Funnels built: landing page, checkout, thank you page
- Automations installed: speed-to-lead SMS, 14-day nurture, reactivation campaign
- Pipelines configured and labeled for their event
- Ticket sales tracker dashboard live
- All SOPs written and delivered in ClickUp
- Sales scripts written and delivered
- QA checklist completed by Shah before handoff to Ops Manager review
- Client walkthrough call: "here's your system, here's how it works"

**Milestone gate:** Client confirms they can navigate the system and understand every piece.

### Phase 3 — Ads Launch (Days 36–50)
**Goal:** Traffic is flowing, leads are coming in, client team is working leads.
- Ad creative briefed and produced
- Meta campaigns set up and launched
- DM automation configured and live
- Lead flow tested end-to-end (lead comes in → speed-to-lead fires → pipeline updates)
- Client team trained on lead management and Kixie/dialer
- Daily monitoring for first 7 days post-launch
- First performance report sent to client

**Milestone gate:** Cost per lead is within target range and client team is actively working leads.

### Phase 4 — Training + Optimization (Days 51–75)
**Goal:** Client's team can run the entire system without ESA. We are coaching, not doing.
- Weekly training calls (Ops Manager runs, Fathom records)
- Sales script coaching: objection handling, follow-up cadence
- Ad optimization: winning creative identified, budget scaling decisions made
- System adjustments made based on real lead data
- Client team accountability: are they showing up to calls, working the pipeline, hitting activity targets?
- Case study documentation begins

**Milestone gate:** Client team is running independently. ESA is in a coaching role only.

### Phase 5 — Handoff + Renewal (Days 76–90)
**Goal:** Clean exit with everything documented, client set up for their next event, and renewal conversation had.
- Full system audit: every piece working, every automation tested
- Playbook for next event delivered (so they can repeat without starting over)
- Dashboard walkthrough and final performance review
- Results summary: leads generated, tickets sold, revenue produced, ROI
- Renewal conversation: next event, upgrade to DFY, or Marketing Pod ($2,497/month)
- Offboarding checklist completed
- Slack channel archived (or kept open if they renew)

**Milestone gate:** Client signs off that they have everything they need. Renewal decision documented.
