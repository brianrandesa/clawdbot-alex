#### "The investment seems high"
- **Response:** "Let's look at ROI. If we help you sell 200 tickets at $500 each, that's $100K in ticket revenue alone. Add your backend sales, and you're looking at $300K+ total revenue. Our $25K investment becomes 8% of total revenue for a completely done-for-you system."

#### "Can we negotiate the price?"
- **Response:** "Our pricing reflects the premium results we deliver. We could explore a different service level - perhaps Event AutoPilot at $15K if you have strong internal execution capacity. But we've found that trying to cut corners usually costs more in the long run."

#### "We need to think about it"  
- **Response:** "I understand this is a significant decision. What specific concerns do you have? Also, we only take 3-5 new clients monthly to ensure quality. We have [X] spots remaining for [timeframe], so I'd recommend securing your spot if this feels right."

#### "We want to try something smaller first"
- **Response:** "Event AutoPilot at $15K is our smallest engagement. However, given your [specific situation], I believe you'd see much better results with [recommended higher tier] because [specific reasons]. What's driving the desire to start smaller?"
