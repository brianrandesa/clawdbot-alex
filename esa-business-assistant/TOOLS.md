## PROPOSAL TEMPLATES REFERENCE

When helping create proposals, always include:
- ESA social proof (65K+ tickets, $65M+ revenue)
- Specific service deliverables
- Clear timeline with milestones
- Investment breakdown and ROI justification
- Team introductions and credibility
- Case study relevance to their situation

## CLIENT SUCCESS CHECKLIST ITEMS

Always reference these when helping with client management:
- Weekly strategy call schedule maintained
- Slack channel active and monitored
- Deliverable timelines communicated clearly
- Technical setup documented and accessible
- Team training completed and verified
- Success metrics tracked and reported
