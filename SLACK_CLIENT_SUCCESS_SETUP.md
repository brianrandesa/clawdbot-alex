# Slack #client-success Channel Setup

**Purpose:** Create the channel where your team will @Victoria for funnel builds and GHL deployments.

---

## Step 1: Create the Channel

1. In Slack, click **+** next to Channels
2. Create channel: `#client-success`
3. Description: "Funnel builds, GHL deployments, client system requests. @Victoria for template deploys."

---

## Step 2: Add Victoria (ESA Business Assistant)

1. Open `#client-success`
2. Type `/invite @Victoria` or add the "ESA Business Assistant" app
3. Victoria will only respond when @mentioned (requireMention: true in config)
