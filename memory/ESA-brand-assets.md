# ESA Brand Assets - Feb 23, 2026

## EXTRACTED FROM BRIAN'S BRAND DOCUMENTS:

### VISION:
"To lead globally in event ticket sales, selling out major arenas and premier events, with a goal of 1,000,000 tickets sold. We strive to revolutionize event experiences through innovation and excellence, setting new standards for connecting people and creating everlasting memories."

### MISSION:
"To equip 10,000 sales reps with top-notch training and opportunities, aiming for unparalleled success in filling arenas and showcasing the top events worldwide. Our dedication to innovation, team achievement, and client satisfaction positions us as the top choice for significant events."

### CORE VALUES:
- **Purpose-Driven:** Committed to enriching audience experiences, every ticket sale serves a greater cause
- **Endless Curiosity:** Constantly innovating to surpass excellence and refine our sales approach
- **Discipline:** Our promise-keeping is our bond, delivering with unwavering integrity
- **Accountability:** Taking full responsibility for our impact on events and client contentment
- **Vulnerability:** Emphasizing honest, heartfelt communication to build trust and partnership
- **Winning:** Fueled by a tenacious spirit to lead and never give up on our industry-leading mission
