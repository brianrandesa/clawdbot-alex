# ESA Client Success Management System
## Client Success Manager Framework - Feb 22, 2026

### CORE MISSION: $5M Revenue Goal Through Client Lifetime Value Maximization

## 1. CLIENT ONBOARDING SYSTEM

### Day 0: Contract Signature
- **Welcome package:** ESA success stories, team introductions, expectations setting
- **Kickoff call scheduling:** Within 48 hours with dedicated success manager
- **Internal handoff:** Sales to CSM with full qualification scorecard data (65-80 pts = VIP treatment)

### Week 1: Foundation Setting
- **Strategy call:** Review event goals, timeline, success metrics
- **Team introductions:** Connect client to James Mungai (ticket sales lead), relevant specialists
- **Baseline metrics:** Current ticket sales, marketing assets, CRM setup
- **90-day roadmap:** Clear milestones and expectations

### Week 2-4: Implementation Launch
- **Weekly check-ins:** Progress tracking, obstacle identification
- **Performance dashboard setup:** Real-time ticket sales, conversion metrics
- **Success benchmark setting:** Based on their qualification score and event type

## 2. SATISFACTION MONITORING SYSTEM

### Health Score Tracking (Weekly):
- **Green (8-10):** Exceeding ticket sales targets, high engagement, positive feedback
- **Yellow (5-7):** Meeting basic targets, some concerns, needs attention
- **Red (0-4):** Missing targets, communication issues, churn risk

### Satisfaction Touchpoints:
- **Week 2:** First milestone check - how's the experience so far?
- **Week 6:** Mid-campaign pulse - on track for goals?
- **Week 10:** Pre-event satisfaction - ready for success?
- **Post-event:** Results review and relationship cementing

### Early Warning Signals:
- Missed check-in calls
- Delayed response to team requests
- Ticket sales 20%+ below projections
- Complaints about communication or process

## 3. UPSELL OPPORTUNITY IDENTIFICATION

### Primary Upsells (Revenue Maximization):
**Backend Sales Coaching** - If they're selling offers at their event:
- Opportunity: Events with $500+ tickets scoring 65+ points
- Pitch timing: When ticket sales hit 70% of target
- Value prop: "We've filled your room, now let's maximize your backend revenue"

**Multi-Event Packages** - For serial event hosts:
- Opportunity: Clients who've hosted 5+ events (qualification scorecard)
- Pitch timing: 2 weeks post successful event
- Value prop: "Annual partnership for all your events with volume discount"

**Done-For-You Marketing Systems** - Beyond ticket sales:
- Opportunity: Clients spending $10K+ on ads but struggling with campaigns
- Pitch timing: When discussing campaign performance issues
- Value prop: "Let us handle your entire marketing ecosystem"

### Secondary Upsells:
- Extended campaign management
- Social media management for events
- Speaker booking services
- Event production consultation

## 4. CHURN PREVENTION SYSTEM

### At-Risk Client Protocol:
**Yellow Health Score Actions:**
- Immediate success manager call within 24 hours
- Issue identification and rapid resolution plan
- Additional team resources if needed
- Weekly (not bi-weekly) check-ins until back to green

**Red Health Score Actions:**
- CEO escalation (Brian Rand involvement)
- Emergency strategy session with full team
- Potential service credits or extensions
- Last-ditch retention offer

### Proactive Churn Prevention:
- **Expectation management:** Clear communication about realistic timelines
- **Regular wins communication:** Weekly progress reports highlighting successes
- **Problem anticipation:** Industry experience to predict and prevent common issues
- **Relationship building:** Multiple touchpoints across ESA team, not just one contact

## 5. CLIENT LIFETIME VALUE MAXIMIZATION

### Tier System Based on Qualification Score:
**Platinum Clients (70-80 points):**
- Direct access to Brian Rand and senior team
- Priority support and faster response times
- Complimentary strategic consultation for future events
- First access to new services and beta features

**Gold Clients (60-69 points):**
- Dedicated success manager with weekly check-ins
- Priority scheduling for team resources
- Quarterly strategic review calls
- Advanced reporting and analytics

**Silver Clients (50-59 points):**
- Standard success management with bi-weekly check-ins
- Standard response times and support
- Monthly progress reports
- Basic analytics and reporting

### Revenue Optimization Strategies:
1. **Contract Value Growth:** Start with smaller events, prove results, scale to bigger events
2. **Service Expansion:** Begin with ticket sales, add backend coaching, marketing systems
3. **Referral System:** Happy clients become case studies and referral sources
4. **Retention Focus:** Cheaper to keep existing clients than acquire new ones

## 6. SUCCESS METRICS & KPIs

### Client Success Metrics:
- **Client Health Score:** Average across all active clients
- **Retention Rate:** % of clients renewing or rebooking
- **Upsell Rate:** % of clients purchasing additional services
- **Net Promoter Score:** Client satisfaction and referral likelihood

### Revenue Impact Metrics:
- **Client Lifetime Value:** Average revenue per client relationship
- **Upsell Revenue:** Additional revenue from existing clients
- **Churn Cost Avoidance:** Revenue saved through successful retention
- **Referral Revenue:** New business from existing client referrals

### Operational Metrics:
- **Response Time:** Average time to respond to client inquiries
- **Issue Resolution Time:** Average time to resolve client concerns
- **Meeting Completion Rate:** % of scheduled check-ins completed
- **Campaign Success Rate:** % of clients hitting their ticket sales goals

## 7. TEAM INTEGRATION

### Internal Coordination:
- **Weekly CSM-Sales Handoff:** New client briefings with qualification details
- **Bi-weekly Operations Sync:** Coordination with Denise Brooks (COO)
- **Monthly Performance Review:** Results analysis with Brian Rand
- **Quarterly Strategy Planning:** Service expansion and process optimization

### Client Communication Protocol:
- **Primary Contact:** Dedicated CSM for relationship management
- **Escalation Path:** CSM → Senior CSM → COO → CEO as needed
- **Technical Issues:** Direct access to specialist team members
- **Strategic Guidance:** Access to senior team based on client tier

This system positions ESA to maximize every client relationship, turning single-event customers into long-term, high-value partnerships that drive us toward the $5M revenue goal.
