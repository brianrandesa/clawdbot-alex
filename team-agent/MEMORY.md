## CLIENT ONBOARDING PROCESS (7 STEPS)

### **Step 1:** Payment Confirmed (Day 0)
- Stripe payment triggers GHL welcome sequence
- Slack channel created: [FirstName]-ESA  
- Onboarding intake form sent
- Kim monitors completion

### **Step 2:** Intake Form Completion (48-72 hrs)
**Required Info:** Event details, ticket pricing, revenue goals, ad account access, audience size, team members, brand assets

### **Step 3:** Kickoff Strategy Call (60 min)
- Denise or Ops Manager leads
- Fathom records everything
- Confirms event details and timeline
- Identifies custom requirements

### **Step 4:** Custom Roadmap Build (24-48 hrs)
- Built from transcript + intake form
- 90-day sprint milestones
- Goes into ClickUp + Slack pinned message

### **Step 5:** Client Sign-Off Required
- Client approves roadmap in Slack
- Build doesn't start until approval
- Scope protection measure

### **Step 6:** ClickUp Project Creation
- Kim creates from approved roadmap
- Client has view access
- Real-time progress visibility

### **Step 7:** Sprint Execution Begins
- Shah/GHL team executes from ClickUp
- Weekly check-ins with Ops Manager
- Kim sends 3x weekly updates (Mon/Wed/Fri)

## 90-DAY SPRINT PHASES

### **Phase 1 - Foundation (Days 1-14)**
**Goal:** Lock and approve everything before build
- Complete intake and kickoff
- Custom roadmap approved
- GHL sub-account created
- ClickUp workspace built
- Event math model completed

### **Phase 2 - System Build (Days 15-35)**
**Goal:** Full GHL system live and QA'd
- Funnels: landing page, checkout, thank you
- Automations: speed-to-lead, nurture sequences
- Pipelines configured for their event
- SOPs and sales scripts delivered
- QA completed before handoff

### **Phase 3 - Ads Launch (Days 36-50)**
**Goal:** Traffic flowing, leads coming in
- Ad creative produced
- Meta campaigns launched
- DM automation live
- Lead flow tested end-to-end
- Team trained on lead management

### **Phase 4 - Training + Optimization (Days 51-75)**
**Goal:** Client team runs independently
- Weekly coaching calls (Fathom recorded)
- Sales script coaching
- Ad optimization
- System adjustments based on data

### **Phase 5 - Handoff + Renewal (Days 76-90)**
**Goal:** Clean exit with renewal conversation
- Full system audit
- Next event playbook delivered
- Results summary and ROI analysis
- Renewal/upgrade discussion
