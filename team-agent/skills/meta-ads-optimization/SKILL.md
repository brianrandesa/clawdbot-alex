## Event-Specific Ad Campaign Architecture

### Campaign Structure for Events
**Campaign 1: Interest-Based Targeting**
- Broad interests related to event topic
- Multiple ad sets testing different interests
- Budget: 60-70% of total spend

**Campaign 2: Lookalike Audiences**
- Based on client's email list/past attendees
- 1%, 2%, 3% lookalike variations
- Budget: 20-30% of total spend

**Campaign 3: Retargeting**
- Website visitors, video watchers, engagers
- Higher budget per result due to warm audience
- Budget: 10-15% of total spend

### Ad Set Optimization Strategy

**Interest-Based Testing:**
- Start with 3-5 core interests per ad set
- $50-100/day per ad set minimum
- Test audience size 500K-2M optimal
- Monitor frequency and adjust targeting

**Lookalike Scaling:**
- Begin with 1% lookalike (most similar)
- Scale to 2% and 3% as performance validates
- Custom audience size minimum 1,000 for effective lookalikes
- Update source audience monthly

**Retargeting Funnels:**
- 7-day website visitors (most recent)
- 14-day video watchers (engaged content)
- Custom audience from email list
- Exclude converted leads to prevent waste

## Creative Testing Framework

### Ad Format Priority
1. **Single Image** - Fastest to produce, easiest to test
2. **Carousel** - Multiple value propositions
3. **Video** - Higher engagement, production intensive
4. **Collection** - Event agenda/speaker showcase

### Creative Elements Testing

**Hook Variations:**
- Question-based hooks ("Are you tired of...?")
- Benefit-focused hooks ("Learn how to...")
- Urgency hooks ("Last chance to...")
- Social proof hooks ("Join 500+ attendees...")

**Visual Testing:**
- Speaker headshots vs. event branding
- Behind-the-scenes vs. professional staging
- Text overlay vs. clean images
- Color psychology for event themes

**Call-to-Action Optimization:**
- "Reserve Your Spot" vs. "Get Tickets"
- "Learn More" vs. "Register Now"
- Urgency ("Limited Seats") vs. Value ("Free Bonus")

## Performance Analysis & Optimization

### Key Metrics Monitoring
**Primary KPIs:**
- Cost Per Lead (CPL) - Target varies by event ticket price
- Click-Through Rate (CTR) - Industry benchmark 1.5%+
- Cost Per Click (CPC) - Monitor for audience fatigue
- Lead Quality Score - Track phone/email completion rates

**Optimization Triggers:**
- **CPL 2x target**: Pause underperforming ad sets
- **CTR <1%**: Update creative or targeting
- **Frequency >3**: Expand audience or refresh creative
- **High CPC + Low CTR**: Audience saturation

### Weekly Optimization Routine
**Monday: Weekend Performance Review**
- Analyze Friday-Sunday performance
- Pause poorly performing ads
- Increase budget on winners

**Wednesday: Mid-Week Adjustments**
- Review Tuesday performance trends
- Test new audiences if current plateauing
- Creative refresh if frequency increasing

**Friday: Week Wrap-Up & Planning**
- Weekly performance summary
- Plan next week's tests and budgets
- Client communication with insights

## Troubleshooting Common Issues

### High Cost Per Lead
**Diagnostic Steps:**
1. **Audience Analysis**: Too narrow or too broad?
2. **Creative Fatigue**: Frequency above 2.5?
3. **Competition**: Event season increasing costs?
4. **Conversion Tracking**: Attribution window issues?

**Solutions:**
- Expand or narrow audience based on performance
- Introduce new creative variants
- Adjust bid strategy (lowest cost vs. bid cap)
- Review landing page conversion optimization

### Low Click-Through Rate
**Common Causes:**
- Creative not standing out in feed
- Audience mismatch with messaging
- Ad fatigue from over-exposure
- Poor mobile optimization

**Optimization Actions:**
- A/B test bold vs. subtle creative designs
- Review audience insights for better messaging
- Implement creative rotation schedule
- Test mobile-first creative formats

### Lead Quality Issues
**Quality Assessment:**
- Phone number completion rates
- Email address accuracy (bounce rates)
- Follow-up call connection rates
- Actual event registration conversion

**Improvement Strategies:**
- Add qualifying questions to lead forms
- Test different value propositions
- Improve lead magnet alignment with event
- Optimize landing page user experience

## Budget Management & Scaling

### Daily Budget Allocation
**Testing Phase (First 7-14 days):**
- Smaller budgets ($25-50/ad set)
- Multiple variations running simultaneously
- Focus on identifying winners

**Scaling Phase (Days 15-45):**
- Increase winner budgets by 20-50% daily
- Maintain testing with 20% of budget
- Monitor for performance degradation

**Optimization Phase (Days 45-90):**
- Focus budget on proven performers
- Minimal testing, maximum efficiency
- Prepare for client handoff documentation

### Scaling Strategies
**Horizontal Scaling:**
- Duplicate winning ad sets with new audiences
- Test similar interests and demographics
- Expand geographic targeting if applicable

**Vertical Scaling:**
- Increase budget on winning ad sets
- Monitor for diminishing returns
- Scale gradually (20% increases maximum)
