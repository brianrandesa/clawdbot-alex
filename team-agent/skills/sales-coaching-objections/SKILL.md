## Common Objections & Responses

### Price Objections

**"That's too expensive for us right now"**
- **Probe:** "Help me understand - what budget were you thinking?"
- **Reframe:** "Let's talk ROI - if this system sells even 50 tickets at $500 each, that's $25K revenue. Your investment is $15K. That's a 67% profit margin."
- **Alternative:** "Would Autopilot Pro work better? We manage the ads risk for you."

**"We need to think about it"**
- **Probe:** "I understand - what specific part needs more consideration?"
- **Urgency:** "The challenge is event timing. If your event is [date], we need to start the 90-day sprint by [date] to hit your goals."
- **Risk reversal:** "What's the cost of not selling out? Venue deposit, speaker fees, your reputation?"

### Trust/Experience Objections

**"How do we know this will work for our industry?"**
- **Proof:** "We've worked with [relevant industry examples] - the fundamentals are the same: speed-to-lead, nurture sequences, and proven sales psychology."
- **Specifics:** "What's unique about your audience that concerns you?"
- **Adaptation:** "That's exactly why we do a custom roadmap - no copy-paste templates."

**"We've tried agencies before and got burned"**
- **Acknowledge:** "I hear that a lot - most agencies are all-service generalists. We ONLY do event sales."
- **Differentiation:** "We're not an agency - we're event specialists. 250+ events, 5 years of only this."
- **Partnership:** "You're not hiring a vendor, you're gaining a partner invested in your event's success."

### Timing Objections

**"Our event isn't for 6 months"**
- **Timeline education:** "Actually perfect - most clients start 90-120 days out. Earlier start means more optimization time."
- **Competitive advantage:** "Your competitors are starting their marketing now for events around that time."
- **Phase benefits:** "The Foundation phase ensures everything is approved before we build anything."

**"We're not ready to commit"**
- **Risk analysis:** "What needs to happen for you to feel ready?"
- **Timeline pressure:** "The risk is waiting too long and rushing the system build."
- **Phase protection:** "That's why Phase 1 is all planning - we don't build until you approve everything."

### DIY/Internal Team Objections

**"We want to do this ourselves"**
- **Skill gap:** "What's your team's experience with GHL automation and Meta ad optimization?"
- **Time reality:** "How many hours per week can your team dedicate to this during the 90 days?"
- **Opportunity cost:** "What revenue-generating activities won't they be doing while building this?"

**"We have a marketing person already"**
- **Collaboration:** "Perfect - they'll be our main training partner. This enhances their capabilities."
- **Specialization:** "Do they specialize in event sales? Different from general marketing."
- **Systems thinking:** "We're not replacing them - we're giving them a proven system to execute."

## Advanced Closing Techniques

### Assumptive Close
**For qualified prospects:**
- "Based on your [event date] and [revenue goal], we'll need to start the Foundation phase by [date]. Should I get you scheduled with our onboarding team?"

### Alternative Close  
**When facing hesitation:**
- "Would you prefer to start with Event AutoPilot and see how it works, or jump into Autopilot Pro so we handle the ads risk for you?"

### Scarcity Close
**When appropriate:**
- "We only take 3-5 new clients per month. For [event month], we have [X] spots remaining."

### Pain Point Close
**For urgent situations:**
- "What happens if your event doesn't sell out? Venue costs, speaker fees, team effort - what's that total exposure?"

## Service-Specific Objections

### DFY Ticket Sales Objections

**"Commission structure seems high"**
- **Value demonstration:** "Our team sells tickets faster than internal teams - velocity matters for events."
- **Risk transfer:** "You only pay when we produce results. Zero risk to you."
- **Opportunity cost:** "How much revenue do you lose per day your team spends selling instead of content creation?"

**"We want to control the sales process"**
- **Collaboration:** "You approve all scripts, objection responses, and positioning."
- **Brand alignment:** "We become an extension of your brand, not replace it."
- **Final authority:** "You maintain approval rights over all client interactions."

### Autopilot Pro Objections

**"Why not just do Event AutoPilot?"**
- **Risk mitigation:** "Ad management is complex - one wrong setting can waste thousands."
- **Learning curve:** "You learn while we execute, then take over with proven results."
- **Timeline:** "6-month engagement means support through your entire launch sequence."

### Event AutoPilot Objections

**"90 days seems short"**
- **Intensity benefit:** "Focused sprints get better results than dragged-out projects."
- **Event timing:** "Events have hard deadlines - we work backward from your event date."
- **Complete handoff:** "Day 91, you own everything and can run the next event independently."
