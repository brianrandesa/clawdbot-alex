# Legal Contract Drafting for ESA

This skill handles all contract-related needs for Event Sales Agency's client relationships across all service tiers.

## Contract Types Supported

### Service Agreements (Primary)
- **DFY Ticket Sales** ($50K-$100K+ plus commission)
- **Autopilot Pro** ($25K / 6 months)  
- **Event AutoPilot** ($15K / 90 days)
- **Marketing Pod** ($2,497/month ongoing)

### Amendment and Modification Documents
- Scope change orders
- Timeline extensions
- Service tier upgrades/downgrades
- Additional deliverable addendums

### Governance Documents
- Refund policy agreements
- Intellectual property ownership clauses
- Data usage and privacy terms
- Termination and transition procedures

## Key Contract Elements

### Service Scope Definition
**Clear deliverable boundaries:**
- What ESA builds vs. what client provides
- GHL system ownership and access rights
- Ad account management responsibilities
- Training and handoff procedures

**Timeline specifications:**
- 90-day sprint milestones with client sign-off requirements
- Response time SLAs for both parties
- Delay consequences and remedies

### Financial Terms
**Payment structure:**
- Upfront fees, milestone payments, commission structures
- Refund policies tied to sprint phases
- Late payment penalties and collection procedures
- Currency and payment method specifications

**Liability limitations:**
- Performance guarantees vs. best-effort clauses
- Force majeure and external dependency protections
- Data loss and system failure liability caps

### Intellectual Property
**ESA-created assets:**
- GHL snapshots, automations, and funnel ownership
- Sales script and training material licensing
- Custom development and integration rights

**Client-provided materials:**
- Brand asset usage permissions
- Lead data ownership and usage rights
- Event content and promotional material licensing

## ESA-Specific Legal Considerations

### Service Tier Escalations
Language supporting natural upgrade path:
- DFY ticket sales performance triggers
- Autopilot Pro transition at 90-day mark
- Marketing Pod continuation options

### Performance Metrics
**Measurable outcomes:**
- Lead generation targets and cost per lead thresholds  
- Ticket sales goals and revenue benchmarks
- System uptime and response time guarantees

**Remedies for underperformance:**
- Additional training and support provisions
- Timeline extensions vs. partial refunds
- Scope adjustment procedures

### Client Responsibility Clauses
**Required cooperation:**
- Asset provision deadlines with consequences
- Decision approval timelines for roadmaps
- Team training participation requirements
- Marketing material approval processes

## Drafting Process

### Information Gathering
1. **Client details:** Company name, key contacts, billing information
2. **Service specifics:** Tier selection, custom requirements, timeline
3. **Event details:** Date, revenue goals, unique circumstances
4. **Risk factors:** Client experience level, timeline constraints, special needs

### Contract Customization
Start with appropriate template, then customize:
- Insert client-specific event math and goals
- Adjust timeline based on event date proximity
- Include custom deliverables from Fathom analysis
- Add specific performance metrics and success criteria

### Review Checkpoints
- ✅ Scope matches approved roadmap exactly
- ✅ Payment terms align with service tier
- ✅ Timeline realistic for event date
- ✅ ESA liability appropriately limited
- ✅ Client responsibilities clearly defined
- ✅ Termination procedures favor ESA interests

## Risk Mitigation Strategies

### Scope Creep Protection
- Detailed deliverable lists with exclusions
- Change order procedures with additional fees
- Client approval requirements before scope expansion

### Payment Security  
- Milestone-based payment releases
- Work stoppage clauses for non-payment
- Collection procedures and jurisdiction specifications

### Performance Protection
- Best effort vs. guarantee language distinction
- External dependency disclaimers (ad platform changes, etc.)
- Client cooperation requirements for success

## Integration with ESA Processes

### Roadmap Synchronization
Contract deliverables must match:
- Custom roadmap from Fathom analysis
- ClickUp project structure and timelines
- Team member assignments and responsibilities

### Client Communication Alignment
Contract language should support:
- Weekly update communication standards
- Sprint health status tracking procedures
- Escalation and intervention protocols
